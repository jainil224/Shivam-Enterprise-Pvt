Trade-offs

 What it isStrengthRisk
A Single continuous S with a short thread-end flick off the top-right Cleanest at 16×16. Fewest strokes, nothing to clog. Most neutral / most reusable Least distinctive. Reads as a generic letterform — the “yarn” cue is only the flick
B S with a second Lavender ply crossing it through the lower bowl Says yarn most clearly — two plies crossing is textile-specific Two-colour and busier. The crossing is the first thing to clog at 16×16
C S whose tail curls into a small loop at the bottom Most ownable. The loop reads unmistakably as a loose thread end Most detail. Highest chance of turning muddy small, and the lowest bowl sits closest to the edge

What happens after you pick

Reply with A, B or C and I will:

  1. Inline the winning mark into the navbar and footer <a class="brand"> and .footer__brand — inlined, not <img>, so it inherits currentColor and inverts to Platinum in the footer for free.
  2. Add aria-hidden="true" focusable="false" so the name is not announced twice.
  3. Change .brand from align-items:baseline to center — baseline alignment with an SVG child sits wrong.
  4. Generate images/favicon.svg and replace the href="data:," stop-404 hack in index.html.
  5. Delete the two losing variant files.
  6. Update design.md and README.md, then re-run the full suite (overflow at all 11 widths, 60/30/10, hex audit).
